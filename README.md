# Breaking Law Platform

## Overview

Breaking Law is legal platform for law firms using FastAPI + AI automation for:
- Drafting legal documents
- Review processes
- Versioning of legal documents
- Client communication
- Time tracking
- Meetings management
- Deadlines tracking
- Court filings
- Hour justification

## Architecture

The platform implements 4 core layers:
1. Ingestion layer
2. Storage/search layer
3. LLM/prompting layer
4. Frontend/delivery layer

## Features

- AI-powered document generation with RAG over firm legal documents
- Prompts by jurisdiction/area specialization
- Anti-hallucination guardrails
- Secure client communication channels
- Time tracking and billing automation
- Deadline management system
- Court filing automation
